# Career Assessment Questions

## Interests

1. What activities do you enjoy most?
2. What subjects do you enjoy learning about?
3. What type of work excites you?

## Strengths

4. What are you naturally good at?
5. What skills do people often praise you for?
6. Do you enjoy working with people, ideas, or systems?

## Work Preferences

7. Do you prefer teamwork or independent work?
8. Do you enjoy leadership roles?
9. Do you prefer routine or variety?

## Career Goals

10. What impact would you like to make?
11. What does success mean to you?
12. What type of lifestyle do you want in the future?
