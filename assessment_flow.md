# Assessment Flow

## Target Audience

University students and fresh graduates who feel confused about their career direction.

---

## Goal

To help users understand their interests, strengths, work preferences, and career values before suggesting suitable career paths.

---

## Assessment Sections

### 1. Interests
Helps identify what the user naturally enjoys.

### 2. Strengths
Helps identify what the user is good at.

### 3. Personality Style
Helps understand how the user works and behaves.

### 4. Work Preferences
Helps identify the environment where the user may perform best.

### 5. Career Values
Helps identify what matters most in the user's future career.

---

## Career Recommendation Logic

### Mostly A Answers
Recommended fields:

- Psychology
- Human Resources
- Teaching
- Counseling
- Social Work
- Training and Development

Reason:

The user shows strong people-oriented interests, empathy, communication, and a desire to guide others.

---

### Mostly B Answers
Recommended fields:

- Software Development
- Artificial Intelligence
- Engineering
- Cybersecurity
- Data Tools
- Product Development

Reason:

The user shows interest in problem-solving, technology, tools, and practical solutions.

---

### Mostly C Answers
Recommended fields:

- Graphic Design
- Content Creation
- Media
- Fashion Design
- Video Editing
- Creative Marketing

Reason:

The user shows creativity, imagination, visual thinking, and interest in self-expression.

---

### Mostly D Answers
Recommended fields:

- Marketing
- Business Development
- Entrepreneurship
- Sales
- Management
- Brand Strategy

Reason:

The user shows leadership, growth mindset, business interest, and confidence in taking charge.

---

### Mostly E Answers
Recommended fields:

- Research
- Statistics
- Finance
- Academia
- Data Analysis
- Operations Management

Reason:

The user shows analytical thinking, curiosity, attention to detail, and interest in deep understanding.

---

## Final Output Example

**People-Oriented Careers**

You may be suitable for:

- Human Resources
- Organizational Psychology
- Career Counseling
- Teaching
- Training and Development

Why:

Your answers show that you enjoy helping people, communicating, guiding others, and making a positive impact.
