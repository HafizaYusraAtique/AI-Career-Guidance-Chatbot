# AI Career Guidance Chatbot

An AI-powered career guidance assistant designed to help students and professionals understand their strengths, interests, and suitable career paths.

---

## Purpose

This project combines Organizational Psychology, Human Resources, and Artificial Intelligence to support better career decision-making.

The chatbot will help users explore:

- Career confusion
- Personal interests
- Strengths and skills
- Suitable fields
- Workplace preferences
- Future career goals

---

## Why This Project Matters

Many students and young professionals feel confused about their future because they do not understand their interests, strengths, or career options.

This chatbot aims to guide them through simple questions and provide helpful career direction.

---

## Key Features

- Career interest questions
- Strength identification
- Skill-based suggestions
- Field recommendations
- Personalized career guidance
- Simple and friendly conversation flow

---

## Psychology-Based Approach

This project is inspired by:

- Organizational Psychology
- Career Counseling
- Personality and Interest Assessment
- Workplace Behavior
- Motivation and Goal Setting
